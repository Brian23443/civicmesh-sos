# RescuNote Terms of Service

Status: Proposed draft for maintainer and legal review; not yet effective or binding.  
Prepared: October 11, 2026. Intended audience: United States of America (USA).

## Service and operator

RescuNote helps survivors prepare compact emergency reports and helps operators view reports received by a bridge. Each deployment must identify its responsible legal operator, contact details, service scope, and effective date before adopting these terms. The product name alone does not identify a contracting party. Adding this file does not establish user acceptance.

## Emergency safety

When possible, contact 911 directly and follow official emergency instructions. RescuNote is not a 911 service, medical diagnosis, dispatch guarantee, or promise of rescue. Connectivity, power, hardware, storage, and location failures may prevent operation. A report marked handed to a relay is not a delivery acknowledgement from a responder.

Automated triage suggestions can be inaccurate. Review the structured report and counts before sending. A third-party report may carry the reporter's GPS location rather than the incident's location; operators must consider this distinction when acting on a report. Demonstration and sample incidents must not be treated as verified emergencies.

## User and operator responsibilities

Submit information honestly to the best of your knowledge. Do not knowingly fabricate emergencies, disrupt the service, misuse another person's information, or access systems without authorization. Report only information reasonably needed for emergency assistance. These expectations do not require a person in distress to guarantee that every detail is accurate.

Operators must validate operational readiness, protect access to bridge and dashboard systems, establish incident-handling and retention procedures, and obey applicable laws. RescuNote does not replace trained human judgment. Users must not assume that installing the app enrolls them with an emergency agency.

## Availability, changes, and ending use

Availability and successful transmission are not guaranteed. Offline use requires assets to be available in advance and compatible equipment; storage eviction or missing files can prevent it. Operators should communicate significant service or policy changes through an accessible channel. Stopping use or clearing browser data does not erase already received reports.

## Legal terms requiring approval

No legal operator, support contact, state governing law, venue, liability cap, arbitration clause, or effective acceptance process has been approved. The country is the United States; this does not select a state's law or displace applicable federal or state protections. Any final disclaimers, suspension provisions, dispute terms, and limitations must be reviewed by qualified counsel and preserve rights that cannot legally be waived.

Before public adoption, replace this draft status with an approved effective date, identify the operator and contact, confirm actual service practices, and implement suitable notice and acceptance where required. Do not rely on this draft as an executed agreement.
