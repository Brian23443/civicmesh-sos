Status: Proposed draft for maintainer and legal review; not yet effective or binding.

## Scope and licensing

This draft addresses use of RescuNote software, including the survivor application and operator dashboard. Before adoption, the rights holder must identify the licensor, verify authority to grant software rights, specify the applicable license, and provide a support contact and effective date. This draft does not itself grant new rights or override existing licenses.

Where repository code, models, fonts, maps, or other dependencies have applicable open-source or third-party licenses, those licenses govern their respective materials. Nothing in this draft restricts rights those licenses grant or waives their attribution requirements. A missing license must not be interpreted as unrestricted permission to copy, redistribute, or commercially use the software.

## Responsible use

Use the software lawfully and do not knowingly submit false emergency reports, interfere with responders, gain unauthorized access to another operator's systems, or expose other people's sensitive report information. Operators are responsible for authorized access, hardware configuration, map/model provisioning, and protecting stored reports. Do not remove notices required by applicable licenses.

## Emergency and technical limitations

RescuNote is not a replacement for 911, a medical professional, or official emergency instructions. When possible, contact 911 directly. Automated labels can be wrong and should be checked before sending. A relay handoff or displayed incident is not confirmation that a responder received the report or that help is coming.

Offline operation depends on previously installed assets, supported browser capabilities, available storage, powered and compatible hardware, and radio reachability. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted. Coordinates and third-party locations can be inaccurate. No guarantee of coverage, availability, delivery, or rescue is made by this draft.

## Warranty, liability, and ending use

Any final warranty disclaimer and liability terms must be reviewed against the applicable software licenses and governing law before adoption. This draft does not set a liability cap, require arbitration, waive class-action rights, or remove non-waivable consumer protections. It does not terminate rights independently granted by an open-source license.

You may stop using RescuNote and remove local software and browser data. This does not delete reports already received by a relay or operator. See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) and [Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md).
